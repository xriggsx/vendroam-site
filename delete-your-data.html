Delete Your Data — VendRoam

Developer: Cunning Creek Studios

VendRoam lets you delete your data directly in the app — no account, email, or waiting period required. Deletion is immediate.

If you’re a customer

You can delete one vendor at a time, or everything at once.

To stop following a single vendor

  1. Open the app and go to My Vendors.
  2. Tap the delete (trash) icon next to the vendor you want to remove.
  3. Tap Stop Following to confirm.

What’s deleted: that vendor is removed from your followed list, and your private registration for it (notification token, last known location, and proximity-ping state) is permanently deleted from our servers immediately.

What’s kept: nothing tied to you for that vendor. Any other vendors you follow are unaffected.

To delete everything at once

  1. Open the app and go to My Vendors → Settings (top right).
  2. Scroll to Danger Zone.
  3. Tap Delete All My Data, then confirm.

What’s deleted: every vendor you follow is removed from your list, and your private registration for each one is permanently deleted from our servers immediately, in a single action.

What’s kept: nothing tied to you across any vendor. You can follow any vendor again later by re-scanning its barcode.

Retention period: none for either option — deletion happens immediately when you confirm, with no grace period.

If you’re a vendor

Steps

  1. Open the app and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Edit, then scroll to Danger Zone.
  3. Tap Delete My Vendor.
  4. Type your business’s exact name to confirm, then tap Delete Forever.

What’s deleted: your vendor profile (name, description, contact details), its menu items and photos, its calendar/event entries, every customer’s follower registration for your vendor, and its recovery code — all deleted permanently and immediately.

What’s kept: nothing. This also immediately removes your vendor from every customer currently following it.

Retention period: none — deletion happens immediately when you confirm, with no grace period.
