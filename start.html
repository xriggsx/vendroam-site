Four steps

  1. 1

    Get the app 1 min

    Install VendRoam from Google Play. Open it, choose I'm a vendor, then Set up a new vendor.

  2. 2

    Set up your business 10 min

    Pick your business type and add your name, a short description and your contact details. Then:

    • Save your recovery code. It's shown once. It moves your business to a new phone if you ever need to.
    • Build your menu in the Menu tab. Photos and prices, with categories you name yourself.
    • Add your private spots in Settings, like your home or where you keep the van. You're never shown on the map inside one.
  3. 3

    Make your counter sign 2 min

    Open the Barcode tab and tap Copy my link. Paste it into the sign maker, then print. You can also share your link on Facebook and Instagram so people can follow you from there.

  4. 4

    Claim your 3 free months 2 min

    Send us your VendRoam link (details below). We'll switch on your free months, usually within a day, and let you know when it's done. Then tap Start Shift and you're live.

Claim your 3 free months
